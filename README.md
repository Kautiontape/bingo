# People Watching Bingo

**https://bingo.kautiontape.com**

Open it anywhere there are people and you get a card of things to spot: a baby in a stroller,
someone picking their nose, sunglasses indoors. Tap what you see. Five in a row wins, and the
confetti doesn't stop until you tell it to.

- **Your own boards.** Write a list, pick 3×3, 4×4 or 5×5, and choose whether the center is free.
- **Play together.** Share a link or QR code. Either everyone gets their own card from your list
  and races to bingo, or everyone marks one shared card together. You see who's playing, who's
  close, and who just won.
- **Make your own.** Like someone's board? Copy the list into a board of your own.

No accounts. Private boards stay in your browser; shared boards are deleted after 90 days
without play.

Built with vanilla JS, Hono and SQLite. See `CLAUDE.md` for development and
`docs/design/` for the design.
